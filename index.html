1. Overall Exam Interface
General Information:
  1. The clock will be set at the server. The countdown timer at the top right corner of the screen will display the remaining time.
  2. The Question Palette displayed on the right side of the screen will show the status of each question using color codes.
  3. To answer a question, click on the question number in the Question Palette.
  4. You can click on Save & Next to save your answer for the current question and then go to the next.

Note: Closing the browser or refreshing the page will not submit your exam, but you may lose progress if the session expires.

2. Question Palette Legend

Refer to these colors to track your progress.

The question palette on the right side of your screen will display the following visual anchors:

  1. White: You have not visited the question yet.
  2. Red: You have visited the question but have not answered it.
  3. Green: You have answered the question.
3. Submitting Responses
  1. To Answer: Click on the radio button next to your desired option.
  2. To Save: You MUST click the "Save & Next" button to save your response before moving to another question. Simply clicking an option without saving will clear your choice.
  3. To Clear: Click the "Clear Response" button if you wish to deselect your chosen answer.
  4. Final Submission: The exam will automatically submit when the timer reaches zero. You can also click the "Submit" button to submit your exam, however we recommend you to stay for full time.
